Intended Use & Limitations

This model artifact is built strictly for investigational academic experimentation and machine-learning benchmarking. The decision threshold was selected by optimizing Youden's J statistic on patient-grouped validation folds. It must not be deployed in triage or bedside care without prospective clinical trial clearance.
